# Assumptions and client validation questions

The MVP assumes a flat UTF-8/Latin-1 CSV, 1–5 rating scales, Yes/No recommendation values, English text, one response per row, and no need to persist raw uploaded data. Current report templates are proposed because client examples have not been supplied. Theme labels are presentation baselines, not final taxonomies. External AI is disabled.

Questions still requiring client confirmation:

1. Can Learning Futures provide anonymised Qualtrics exports and the survey question structure?
2. Can the team receive a sample manually generated evaluation report?
3. Which metrics matter most?
4. What specifically distinguishes facilitator and commercial-client reports?
5. How is report quality assessed, and how long does manual reporting take?
6. Which Copilot/Microsoft environment, Power Automate and Power BI capabilities are available?
7. What restrictions apply to storage, processing, retention and approved tools?
8. Is historical comparison required, and which deliveries are comparable?
9. Who is authorised to approve outputs?
10. What would make the prototype valuable enough to continue developing?
